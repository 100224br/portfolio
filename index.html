About Me

My story as a software developer and my passion for technology.

Hello! I am Brandon Wijngaarde, a passionate software development student at Grafisch Lyceum Rotterdam. My journey in the world of programming began with curiosity about how websites and applications work, and has grown into a real passion for creating digital solutions.

During my studies, I have immersed myself in various technologies, from frontend development with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, to backend development with PHP and SQL. I am also actively working with React and TypeScript for modern web applications, and have gained experience with C# and Unity for game development.

What appeals to me most in software development is the creative challenge and the ability to solve problems with code. I enjoy experimenting with new technologies and continuously expanding my skills. My goal is to become a versatile developer who can develop both frontend and backend.

In addition to my studies, I like to work on personal projects to put my knowledge into practice. I am always looking for new challenges and enjoy learning from other developers in the community.
